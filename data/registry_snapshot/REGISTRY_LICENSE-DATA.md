## Not covered

- Paper metadata harvested from arXiv and INSPIRE-HEP (titles, authors,
  abstracts, identifiers and similar fields, mostly in `missing_papers/`)
  stays under the terms set by those services.
- Short quotations from papers in the `quote_or_summary` fields of the cards
  remain the work of the papers' authors; they are there to cite the source
  of each claim.

Card content is machine-extracted from paper full texts and is not yet
expert-verified. The material is provided as is, without warranty, as
CC BY 4.0 states.
